## What's new

- **Your own buttons.** The two buttons on the Controls tab can now do what you like: Snip, Colour, Calculator, Files, Task Manager, Settings, Lock, Clipboard, or open any app or website. Set them in Settings > Shortcuts.
- **Your own shortcut keys.** Click a shortcut's keys in Settings > Shortcuts and press new ones.
- **Choose what Play opens.** When nothing is playing, Play can start Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music or your own app or website. Set it in Settings > Music Apps.
- **Covers that always show.** If a music app is slow to send a song's cover, Crest keeps asking. If the app leaves it out altogether, Crest can find it online by the song's title and artist (only those two are sent, to Apple's iTunes search; turn it off in Settings > Now Playing).
- **Fixed:** the island hid itself on the desktop and in the Settings app, as if they were fullscreen.
- **Support Crest:** Crest is free and always will be. If you like it, you can now [sponsor it on GitHub](https://github.com/sponsors/Namanjeet13).

This update installs itself while you aren't using the island.
