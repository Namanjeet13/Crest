## What's new

- **Updates from inside Crest.** Crest now checks GitHub for new versions and tells you when one is out. Install it from Settings > About: Crest downloads it, checks it against GitHub's published checksum, updates itself and opens again. You can turn the check off. This is the last version you need to download by hand.
- **Discord voice controls.** While you're in a Discord voice channel, open the island to mute, deafen or leave the channel. It works while Discord is minimized or closed to the tray, and you don't need to link an account or add a bot. The closed island turns red when you're muted, and shows crossed-out headphones when you're deafened. You can turn this off in Settings > Calls.
- **Pixel pet.** A cat, dog or bunny can live in the idle island. It blinks, falls asleep when you're away and shows hearts when you rest the pointer on it. Pick it, its size and its corner in Settings > Pet.
- **Move the island.** Drag it along the top edge of your screen; it remembers where you left it.
- **Play starts Spotify.** If nothing is playing, pressing play opens Spotify minimized and resumes your last song.
- **Fixed:** the call timer could show hundreds of hours after Discord updated or crashed during a call.
- **Security:** "jump to the app" only ever opens plain app IDs, and the uninstaller only runs from Crest's own install folder.

Discord voice controls need Discord's language set to English.
