## What's new

- **Widgets on the closed island.** Show a clock, the date, battery, CPU, memory, network speed, a countdown, day progress, uptime or your own text beside the notch. Each widget has its own options. Pick them in Settings > Widgets.
- **Redesigned settings.** Fewer, clearer pages, plainer wording, a search box that finds any setting, and a slim scroll bar.
- **Smoother animations.** Set how fast the island opens and closes, in milliseconds. The stutter when it opened is fixed.
- **Hold Esc in fullscreen.** With "Hide in fullscreen" on, hold Esc (even in a game) to bring the island back and free the pointer while you hold it.
- **Live liquid glass.** The open island now shows what's behind it live instead of a captured frame, and the glass edge blends softly into the frost.
- **Inter typeface** throughout the island and settings.
- **Lighter and faster.** Crest gives memory back after you close Settings, and old unused code is gone.
